陷阱 1：正常財富來源Trap 1: normal sources of wealth

0302 有遺產繼承、0308 是離婚和解。這些都是正常來源，模型不該因此判高風險。0302 includes an inheritance and 0308 a divorce settlement. Both are ordinary sources; the model should not flag them as high risk.

陷阱 2：複雜不等於危險Trap 2: complex is not risky

0309 主權基金、0310 家族辦公室作業很重，但沒有洗錢警訊。分數混在一起就會判錯。0309 (sovereign fund) and 0310 (family office) are heavy to onboard but show no laundering red flags. Mixing the two scores gets them wrong.

陷阱 3：「單純案件」Trap 3: “straightforward”

好幾筆的描述寫著 straightforward，那是客戶或 RM 的說法，不能直接當證據。Several descriptions call the case straightforward. That is the client’s or the RM’s word, not evidence.
